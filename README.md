📰project name: বাজার দর
 
💠description: বাজার দর is a daily market price tracking website designed to help people make smarter purchasing decisions in their everyday lives.

The main goal of this platform is to provide users with updated market prices of daily essential products, allowing them to understand current market conditions and compare prices before purchasing.

Users can explore today's prices, compare them with previous prices (yesterday, last week, and last month), and check market-wise price variations.

By analyzing historical price changes over the last month, users can understand whether a product's price is increasing, decreasing, or remaining stable. This information helps them decide whether to buy a product now or wait for a potentially better price.
 
⚙technologies:
   1. Nextjs
   2. Better Auth
   3. Mongodb 
   4. HeroUI
   5. Marque
   6. Tailwind CSS
   7. TypeScript

🎡features:
 1. User Authentication: Add Sign In and Sign Up pages to allow users to create accounts and securely access the platform.

2. User Profiles: Allow registered users to manage their personal information.
 Today's Market Prices: View updated prices of daily essential products.

3. Price History: Compare today's prices with yesterday, last week, and last month.

4. Market-wise Comparison: Compare product prices across different local markets.

5. Price Trends: Understand whether product prices are increasing or decreasing.

6. Product Categories: Explore products by category.

7. Smart Purchasing Decisions: Help consumers decide when and where to buy products.
